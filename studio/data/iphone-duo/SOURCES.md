## Claims

### e-announce
Apple introduced iPhone Duo on September 9, 2026, in Cupertino, and called it the first foldable iPhone.

Source: s-apple-newsroom. Reliability: primary.

Passage: PRESS RELEASE September 9, 2026. CUPERTINO, CALIFORNIA. Apple today introduced iPhone Duo, the first foldable iPhone.

Limitation: This is the company's description of its own product, not an independent durability test.

### e-displays
Opened, Apple says it is the thinnest iPhone ever, with a 7.6-inch inner display. Closed, the outer display is 5.4 inches.

Source: s-apple-newsroom. Reliability: primary.

Passage: Opened, it is the thinnest iPhone ever, with a 7.6-inch inner display. Closed, the 5.4-inch outer display delivers 90 percent of the screen area of iPhone 18 Pro.

Limitation: Footnote 1 says the rectangular measurements are 7.58 and 5.36 inches and the viewable area is smaller.

### e-area
Apple says the outer display has 90 percent of the screen area of iPhone 18 Pro, and the inner display is 50 percent larger than iPhone 18 Pro Max.

Source: s-apple-newsroom. Reliability: primary.

Passage: Closed, the 5.4-inch outer display delivers 90 percent of the screen area of iPhone 18 Pro. Opened, the 7.6-inch inner Super Retina XDR display is 50 percent larger than iPhone 18 Pro Max.

Limitation: Apple does not publish the iPhone 18 Pro area used as the denominator in this press release.

### e-size
Closed size is 84.1 mm by 117.8 mm by 11.3 mm. Open size is 164.6 mm by 117.8 mm by 5.2 mm. Weight is 254 grams either way.

Source: s-apple-specs. Reliability: primary.

Passage: Open: Width 164.6 mm, Height 117.8 mm, Depth 5.2 mm, Weight 254 grams. Closed: Width 84.1 mm, Height 117.8 mm, Depth 11.3 mm, Weight 254 grams.

Limitation: Spec-sheet dimensions. No third-party caliper measurement was read.

### e-passport
Apple says the closed phone is similar in size to a passport.

Source: s-apple-newsroom. Reliability: primary.

Passage: Closed, it’s similar in size to a passport with a beautiful 5.4-inch outer display.

Limitation: A simile, not a measured passport standard.

### e-aspect
Apple says both displays share the same aspect ratio. The published pixel grids are 1878 by 2670 at 430 ppi and 1398 by 2034 at 460 ppi.

Source: s-apple-specs. Reliability: primary.

Passage: Both displays share the same aspect ratio. Inner display: 1878-by-2670-pixel resolution at 430 ppi. Outer display: 1398-by-2034-pixel resolution at 460 ppi. Footnote: screens are 7.58 and 5.36 inches diagonally (actual viewable area is less).

Limitation: 1878/2670 and 1398/2034 are not the same ratio. Rounded corners mean the pixel grid is not the viewable aspect.

### e-crease
Counterpoint wrote on May 12, 2026 that crease work had shifted from the hinge alone to material stiffness, thickness, and stress release.

Source: s-counterpoint-year. Reliability: secondary.

Passage: The key to crease improvement has shifted from hinge mechanism design to the synergistic coordination of material modulus, thickness distribution, and stress release. Published 2026-05-12.

Limitation: Analyst framing. The page is a report summary, not the full paid dataset.

### e-stack
Apple describes a nano-textured custom polymer cover, up to 40 percent higher stiffness than other industry materials, glass above and below the panel, gliding adhesives, and a titanium plate.

Source: s-apple-newsroom. Reliability: primary.

Passage: A nano-textured cover layer — made from a custom polymer with up to 40 percent higher stiffness than other materials used in the industry. High-strength glass sits above and below the folding display panel, with custom adhesives that allow the layers to glide past one another like the pages of a book, and a titanium plate at the bottom.

Limitation: The comparison does not name the other materials.

### e-hinge
Apple says the hinge has more than 100 components, carbon fiber support plates, holds the display flat when open, and uses a magnet array to close.

Source: s-apple-newsroom. Reliability: primary.

Passage: The hinge is engineered from more than 100 components. The hinge has over a hundred tiny components with carbon fiber support plates. It supports the center of the display to hold it flat when opened, and together with an integrated magnet array, delivers a satisfying and secure closure.

Limitation: A part count, not a published cycle-life test.

### e-recycle
Apple says the 3D-printed hinge cover is 100 percent recycled titanium, and 35 percent of device mass is recycled content, including 100 percent recycled cobalt in the battery.

Source: s-apple-newsroom. Reliability: primary.

Passage: It is made with 35 percent recycled content overall, including 100 percent recycled cobalt in the battery and 100 percent recycled titanium in the 3D-printed hinge cover. Footnote 11: recycled content is the mass of certified recycled material relative to the overall mass of the device, not including packaging.

Limitation: Excludes packaging and in-box accessories. Certification details are not in the press release.

### e-shield
The frame is grade 5 titanium. Ceramic Shield 2 is claimed at 3 times the scratch resistance of the previous generation. IP68 is a lab rating of 6 meters for up to 30 minutes under IEC 60529, and Apple says it is not permanent.

Source: s-apple-newsroom. Reliability: primary.

Passage: Crafted from grade 5 titanium. Ceramic Shield 2 on the front has 3x better scratch resistance than the previous generation. IP68 under IEC standard 60529 (maximum depth of 6 meters for up to 30 minutes). Resistance might decrease as a result of normal wear.

Limitation: Lab conditions. Scratch claim is versus Apple's previous generation, not a named rival glass.

### e-chip
A20 Pro is the same chip as in iPhone 18 Pro models. Apple says it is 2-nanometer, with a 6-core CPU up to 20 percent faster than A19 Pro, a 7-core GPU up to 40 percent faster, two 16-core Neural Engines with 2x the compute, and 50 percent more unified memory bandwidth.

Source: s-apple-newsroom. Reliability: primary.

Passage: Powered by A20 Pro — the same chip in iPhone 18 Pro models. The 6-core CPU is up to 20 percent faster than A19 Pro. The 7-core GPU is up to 40 percent faster. The new Dual 16-core Neural Engine offers 2x the compute power. A20 Pro also delivers 50 percent more unified memory bandwidth and is built on the latest 2-nanometer process technology.

Limitation: Vendor performance claims. Test method for the percentages is not in the pages read.

### e-vapor
Apple says a chip package inspired by M-series silicon connects A20 Pro to a custom vapor chamber, and quotes up to 35 percent better sustained performance than iPhone 17 Pro.

Source: s-apple-newsroom. Reliability: primary.

Passage: iPhone Duo delivers up to 35 percent better sustained performance than iPhone 17 Pro. A new chip package inspired by M-series Apple silicon connects A20 Pro directly to a custom vapor chamber.

Limitation: Comparison is against iPhone 17 Pro, under Apple's conditions. No independent lab result was read.

### e-battery
One battery sits on each side. Apple quotes up to 44 hours of video on the outer display, 31 hours on the inner display, and 24 hours of mixed use when both displays are used equally.

Source: s-apple-newsroom. Reliability: primary.

Passage: One high-energy battery on each side. Up to 31 hours of video playback when using the inner display, up to 44 hours of video playback when using the outer display, and up to 24 hours of usage per charge when both displays are used equally. Footnote 7: testing in August 2026 using preproduction units, LTE and 5G. Typical use based on equal usage between inner and outer displays. Battery life varies.

Limitation: Preproduction units. The 24 hour figure is mixed use, not video playback.

### e-charge
Apple quotes about 50 percent charge in 20 minutes wired with a 60-watt-capable adapter, or about 30 minutes wirelessly with MagSafe or Qi2. The phone is eSIM-only worldwide.

Source: s-apple-newsroom. Reliability: primary.

Passage: Charge up to 50 percent in around 20 minutes or wirelessly charge up to 50 percent in around 30 minutes with a MagSafe or Qi2 charger. Footnote 8: testing in July 2026, drained units in open configuration, 60W-capable adapter. eSIM-only design worldwide that saves internal space to maximize battery capacity.

Limitation: Open, drained preproduction units. Wireless time needs a separate MagSafe charger.

### e-year-share
Counterpoint's May 12, 2026 report says 2025 foldable shipments were 19.6 million, up 18 percent, and 1.6 percent of global smartphone shipments.

Source: s-counterpoint-year. Reliability: secondary.

Passage: In 2025, foldable smartphone shipments achieved 19.6 million units, growing 18.0% YoY. Foldables accounted for only 1.6% of total global smartphone shipments in 2025. Published 2026-05-12.

Limitation: Full report tables were not in the public summary. This is not an iPhone Duo sales figure.

### e-quarter-share
Counterpoint's December 2, 2025 note says Q3 2025 foldable shipments rose 14 percent year over year and were 2.5 percent of smartphone shipments that quarter.

Source: s-counterpoint-q3. Reliability: secondary.

Passage: Global foldable smartphone shipments grew 14% YoY in Q3 2025. Foldable smartphone share reached 2.5% during the quarter. The note is dated December 2, 2025. Book-type models were fueled by Samsung’s Galaxy Z Fold7.

Limitation: A quarter is not the year. Written before the full-year 1.6 percent figure.

### e-price
Apple's U.S. starting price is 1999 dollars for 256GB, or 83.29 dollars a month for 24 months. Storage options include 512GB, 1TB, and 2TB. Colors are star white and night sky.

Source: s-apple-newsroom. Reliability: primary.

Passage: Starts at 1999 dollars (U.S.), or 83.29 dollars per month for 24 months, in 256GB, 512GB, 1TB, and 2TB. Available in star white and night sky. Financing footnote 13 requires credit approval.

Limitation: The monthly figure is financing, not a lower cash price.

### e-verge-price
The Verge wrote that 1999 dollars was 100 dollars more than the Galaxy Z Fold 8 and 100 dollars less than the Z Fold 8 Ultra.

Source: s-verge. Reliability: secondary.

Passage: It starts at 1999 dollars — 100 dollars more than Samsung’s Galaxy Z Fold 8, but 100 dollars less than its Z Fold 8 Ultra — for 256GB. The Verge article is dated September 9, 2026.

Limitation: Samsung's own price page was not opened for this project.

### e-launch
Pre-orders began at 5 a.m. PT on October 16, 2026, with availability on October 23, in more than 70 countries, and 28 more on October 30.

Source: s-apple-newsroom. Reliability: primary.

Passage: Pre-order iPhone Duo beginning at 5 a.m. PT on Friday, October 16, with availability beginning Friday, October 23. More than 70 countries and regions, including Australia, Brazil, Canada, China, Colombia, France, Germany, India, Japan, Malaysia, Mexico, Singapore, South Korea, Türkiye, the UAE, the UK, the U.S., and Vietnam. iPhone Duo will be available in 28 other countries and regions beginning Friday, October 30.

Limitation: A launch schedule. Not a list of factories or retail counts.

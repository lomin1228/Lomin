# Marking Criteria + Distinction Strategy

Source: `bpln0041/PG Assessment Criteria - essays and dissertations.pdf` (UCL PG marking criteria, pass mark 50%).

## The bands
| Grade | Mark | Class |
|---|---|---|
| A+ | 80–100 | Distinction |
| A | 70–79.99 | Distinction |
| B | 60–69.99 | Merit |
| C | 50–59.99 | Pass |
| D–F | <50 | Fail |

## What an A (70–79.99, Distinction) requires — verbatim
- "Presents an **exceptionally clear argument**;"
- "**Integrates a range** of materials and information sources;"
- "Has a **depth of insight** into the theoretical issues and displays **analytical rigour**;"
- "Displays **originality** in treatment and exposition of subject;"
- "Has **perceptive conclusions**."

## What an A+ (80–100) adds — verbatim
- "Makes **exemplary connections** between different areas of the curriculum;"
- "**Synthesises, integrates and critiques a wide range** of evidence and information sources;"
- "Demonstrates an **exemplary understanding** of the subject in a conceptual and imaginative way;"
- "Is of **potentially publishable quality**."

## How THIS dissertation hits each criterion
| Criterion | How we satisfy it |
|---|---|
| Exceptionally clear argument | Single thesis: *market-led delivery degrades the publicness of regenerated public space; viability is the mechanism.* RQ + 4 objectives drive every chapter. |
| Integrates a range of sources | Theory (Peck/Theodore, Harvey) + measurement (Németh & Schmidt, Varna & Tiesdell, Carmona) + mechanism (RICS, Foye, McAllister) + primary (GLA/Southwark docs, field observation) + quant (index, hedonic, DAT). |
| Depth + analytical rigour | The viability mechanism *explains* (not just describes) the publicness pattern; replicable scoring with limitations stated. |
| Originality | Your **"public benefit delivery hierarchy"** operationalised + combined with published indices = a new instrument. Transposing Clifford's housing-quality thesis to *public space*. |
| Perceptive conclusions | Adjudicate the **Minton (pessimist) vs Carmona (pragmatist)** debate with your own data; targeted policy recommendations. |
| Exemplary connections (A+) | Links planning theory ↔ land economics/viability ↔ urban design ↔ political economy — i.e. across your whole MPlan curriculum. |
| Publishable quality (A+) | A clean comparative publicness dataset + the hierarchy-as-method is genuinely publishable (cf. your supervisor's RICS/Urban Studies outputs). |

## Anti-patterns the markers penalise (from the exam criteria)
- Generic/descriptive writing "that could be AI generated" — avoid; always *critique*, don't summarise.
- Reading only from slides — the criteria explicitly reward "critical engagement with appropriate readings... **beyond** the content in slides."
- Uncited overclaims; unresolved discrepancies in figures (see the One Blackfriars caveats in `06`).
